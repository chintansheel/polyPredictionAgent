Why we earn the trust

Forecasts that stand up to scrutiny.

Foretell isn't a tipster. It isn't a hype channel. It isn't a black box pretending to be smart. It's an analysis agent designed to be checked.

01

Every claim cites a source.

Every signal the agent uses links back to the filing, article, or dataset it came from. Click through, verify, disagree. The chain is the product.

02

The track record is public.

Every forecast Foretell makes is timestamped and logged. We don't delete the misses. You can audit the agent's hit rate by domain, horizon, and confidence band.

03

Confidence is calibrated.

A 70% confidence call means roughly seven out of ten calls at that confidence resolve in the predicted direction. We measure ourselves against that, publicly.

04

We update when reality does.

New filing drops at 9.04pm? The forecast moves by 9.05pm. You'll see exactly which signal flipped the call. No stale verdicts, no overnight gaps.
